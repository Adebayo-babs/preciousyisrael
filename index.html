His story

A life given to worship

Precious Yisrael is a worshipper, a pastor and a missionary. He sings so people can feel the presence of God.

Born Precious Nwaka, he first recorded in Lagos in 1991, a six-track demo that started everything. Ten years later, as a 200-level university student, he released his first album. Its songs, led by The Most Important Thing, spread across campus and gave him his first real audience.

A year later came Stand Your Ground. The album received heavy television and radio airplay in Delta, Edo, Anambra, Abia, Bayelsa and neighbouring states, and songs like Ejiro Oghene and Redeemer became familiar in churches across the South-South and South-East.

In 2007 he released I Am Not for the World on audio and video, with his wife, Dorcas, singing on Chineke Idi Nma. Hero followed in 2011, then a run of video singles: Forgive in 2014, I Praise You recorded in Accra, Ghana in 2021, and Faithful You Are in Lagos in 2024.

Along the way he has served in choirs, led as a music director and written songs that congregations still sing. In 2026 he released the video duet One Love. One Peace., which includes Nara Ekele, and in November followed it with the single One Love.

My mission is to reveal the presence of God through Spirit-filled melodies, and to lead people into a deeper walk with Christ.
